# Intent: plan

Goal: learn how the presentation should be structured, from files that describe it (outlines, briefs, agendas, speaker notes, an existing deck to restructure, requirements).

## What to look for
- Audience, objective, desired outcome, tone, duration or slide-count guidance, language, scope and exclusions (working/presentation/brief.md fields).
- Storyline: section order, key messages, requested slides and their intended content.
- Constraints: mandatory sections, branding rules, items to avoid.
- For an existing PPTX: slide order, titles, layouts used and speaker notes, as structure to rework, not as final content.

## Method
- Separate **stated requirements** (explicit in the file) from **your inferences**. Label which is which.
- Where the file references facts or data, those belong under the knowledge intent; propose that intent too instead of embedding facts in the plan.
- Compare with brief.md and slide-plan.json. Flag conflicts with approved content or approved plan revisions; do not overwrite them.
- Propose slide entries with template/forms/slide-entry.json (status draft). Do not assign IDs by guess; follow workflows/00-conventions.md.

## Proposal targets
brief.md fields, new or changed slide-plan.json entries, review-notes.md items, open questions.
